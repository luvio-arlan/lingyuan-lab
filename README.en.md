# Lingyuan Lab · 灵鸢实验室

![Lingyuan Lab logo](assets/logo/lingyuan-wordmark.svg)

**Researching Work and Organizations in the Age of AI**

> Exploring how work changes and organizations evolve with AI.

Lingyuan Lab is an open, evolving research project on the intersection of organization development (OD), AI, and the future of work. It starts with foundational questions, studies evidence and real work, and tests ideas about how AI changes tasks, collaboration, decisions, organizational design, and talent systems.

## The central question

**When AI participates in knowledge work, how should organizations redesign work and coordination?**

Our five research tracks are:

1. **OD foundations:** What is an organization, and what does OD actually change?
2. **Anatomy of work:** How do tasks, roles, workflows, and value creation relate?
3. **Human–AI collaboration:** How should judgment, delegation, and accountability be shared?
4. **Organization design:** How might team boundaries, decision rights, and coordination evolve?
5. **Talent and HR:** What changes in capabilities, development, performance, careers, and employee experience?

## How we work

Each topic begins with a question. We connect foundational theory, current research, observed practice, and explicit hypotheses. Publications distinguish **evidence, case observations, interpretations, and open hypotheses**. Organizational cases are anonymized before publication.

The first outputs are explainers, reading notes, research essays, case analyses, and design hypotheses. The project grows through public learning and careful revision.
